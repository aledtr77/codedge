Starting questions:

- How did you complete this task last time?
- Which step was hardest, and what happened as a result?
- What do you use today, and who chooses which tools to buy?
- Have you looked for an alternative? What happened?

Listen to the process before introducing a solution. Keep only relevant information, avoiding unnecessary confidential data. Ten contacts can be a working goal rather than a representative market sample.

### Essential calculations

Conversion between stages = people at the next stage / people at the previous stage.

Overall conversion = customers / relevant visits.

Attributed spending per customer = attributed spending / new customers.

A zero denominator makes a ratio undefined. Use the same cohort, observation window and genuinely nested stages. Adapt the sequence if your product sells before activation or requires no account. Show counts alongside percentages. Full costs include work and other relevant expenses.
