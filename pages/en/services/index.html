01

02

Browser tools and PWAs

Small interactive applications that run in the browser and, when it makes sense, can be installed. Calculators, timers, utilities and focused workflows that save data on the device or support offline use.

We start with one clear job, not an open-ended list of ideas for a huge product.

03

Web-based Android apps

For projects that suit web technologies inside an Android app, I can work on the interface, agreed features, device integration and preparing the build.

Specialised Android features or a complex backend need to be assessed first; I do not assume they are included.

04

Improvements to existing sites and apps

Defined changes to a project already underway: fixing a page on mobile, adding a component or form, correcting a behaviour, improving accessibility or preparing a change for release.

I first review the code, access and goal. If I cannot make a sensible change, I will say so.

Getting started

First we understand the work. Then we decide whether to do it.

  1. You explain the goal

    What the site or app should do, who it is for and when you need it.

  2. We define the scope

    I review the requirements and suggest what to include, what can wait and how much work it will take.

  3. We build and review

    I work in parts you can review, take your feedback and make changes within the agreed scope.

  4. I hand over the result

    A published website or an app ready for the agreed next step, with source files and useful notes.

A useful boundary

I do not take on every kind of project.

I work independently and do not claim to specialise in every technology. If a requirement needs experience or infrastructure I cannot provide, I will tell you before accepting the work.
