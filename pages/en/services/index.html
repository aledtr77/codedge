Method

How I work on the project.

I share previews as the work progresses and check changes in the browser. I also use AI to explore alternatives and review text and code, then verify the results and decide what to include.

  • Understand

    I ask about your goal, the people who will use the project, the content and any constraints. This helps me understand whether I can help.

    Output A shared scope

  • Define

    I define the structure, features and priorities, and put in writing what is included in the project and what is not.

    Output A clear direction

  • Build

    I prepare previews for you to try and comment on. In the browser, I check the features involved, the interactions and how the layout adapts to different devices.

    Output A project you can try

  • Deliver

    I check the agreed features and invite you to try them. I help with publication and prepare guidance for using the project and managing the planned updates.

    Output Live project and instructions
