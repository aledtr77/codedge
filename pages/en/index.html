CODEDGE

Hi! I'm Alessandro DTR, and CODEDGE is my personal corner of the web, built to share notes, resources and programming experiments. My own path started in front-end, but you'll find a lot more here: an open look at everything orbiting web development and digital technologies. Every topic starts from a quick, no-nonsense summary, for when you want a concept explained as simply and directly as possible; from there we go deep into the code, turning tutorials and ideas into structured, real projects (a construction site in constant evolution, which I'm building day after day).

I've organized the site to give you practical support whatever your level. If you need to clear up a quick doubt about syntax or theory, the Resources section works as your glossary. If you'd rather get your hands dirty, Tools has quick utilities for everyday work — optimizing images or generating palettes, say — while UI Components lets you explore the code behind real elements, ready to study and drop into your own projects. If you're after a stronger through-line, Tutorials offers hands-on paths focused on complete workflows, like Git or publishing online, alongside the ready-to-use layouts in Templates. Not much abstract theory, plenty of code to chew on and try for yourself.
