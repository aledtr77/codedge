Two progressive web apps

A progressive web app is a web application you can install from the browser. Its manifest defines its name, icons and launch behaviour; a service worker can cache pages and assets for offline use. You still need a connection to load content that has not been cached.

Here are two public examples: Codedge, the site you are visiting, lets you reopen the home page and pages you have already loaded offline; GymLog is a training journal that stores data on your device and lets you log sessions without a connection. Both projects are on GitHub.

Bilingual site · Installable

Codedge

Codedge is a PWA: its manifest lets you add it to your home screen and launch it in a standalone window. The service worker caches the home page and, after an online visit, pages and assets from this site. Without a connection, you can reopen what is already cached; a page you have never visited may still need the network.

Training journal · Offline-first

GymLog

GymLog is an installable PWA for planning workouts, logging sets and reviewing progress. After the first load, its cached app shell lets it start offline; workouts and settings stay in the browser on your device, with no account or server sync. You can export a backup to keep a copy of your data.
