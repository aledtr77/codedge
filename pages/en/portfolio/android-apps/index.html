Android app · Available on Google Play

Tallero

A converter for over 160 currencies, with a built-in calculator, favourites and saved rates for converting without a connection. The date and status of the rates remain visible; you can customise the theme, colours and main screen controls.

The app is free to download and requires no account. The current version includes banner ads; an ad-free Premium option is in development.

Android app · Preview · Closed testing

Mirror Time

A timer for organising focus sessions and breaks, with adjustable durations, routines and tasks linked to sessions. Statistics help you review time spent on tasks; settings and history stay on your device.

The app is currently in closed testing. Public availability depends on the test results, and no release date is confirmed yet.
