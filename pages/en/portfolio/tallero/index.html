Tallero, an Android currency converter

Convert between more than 160 currencies, even without a connection, in an interface you can arrange around the way you work.

Choose a starting and target currency, enter an amount and see the result immediately. Use the built-in calculator, keep your most-used currencies close and always see the date and status of the rates in use. Tallero is free and requires no account.

In the app

The tools you need for a clear conversion

Amounts and calculations

Edit either the starting or converted amount and the other updates. The built-in calculator lets you work out values without leaving the app; quick amounts and the swap control make repeated checks faster.

Currencies within reach

Search more than 160 currencies by name, ISO code or symbol. Save the ones you use most as favorites, reorder them and reach them from the main screen. You can also choose your default starting currency.

Conversions while offline

Tallero keeps the latest valid rates on your device. On a first launch without a connection, it can use reference rates included with the app. The data date and status stay visible; once online, you can request an update.

Personalization

Keep the controls you actually use

Choose a light, dark or system theme and one of six accent colors. On the conversion screen you can show quick amounts or favorite currencies, or hide the shortcuts. You can also move or hide the currency swap button.

The interface is available in 49 languages, following your device language or a manual choice. You can also set the date format and adjust keypad sounds and vibration.

About the rates

Tallero uses reference rates from Frankfurter, not live market quotes. Saved values let you keep converting offline, but may be older; your bank or card may apply a different rate.
