About

My name is Alessandro DTR, and Codedge is where I collect everything I study, build and refine in front-end. It's a personal project born to give shape to my own path, pairing a passion for IT and digital technologies with an orderly, quality-driven way of working.

The approach I follow is built on learning by doing: start from the fundamentals and turn them straight into real pages, components or tools. I prefer a concrete path, where testing, revising and improving step by step serve both to consolidate my own skills and to make every section of the site genuinely useful to whoever lands on it.

This space sits alongside Tasto Destro, my YouTube channel where I offer a first taste of web development, digital tools and artificial intelligence. The videos give a quick, approachable overview, meant as a light guide for anyone starting out without excessive complications, while the practical details and the condensed solutions are gathered here on the site.

If you want to find your way around Codedge, you can start from the Tutorials for ready-to-use practical solutions, explore the everyday Tools, or browse the Resources for a quick refresher on syntax and key concepts.
